Midnight Mountain Scene

My SVG scene shows a peaceful mountain landscape at night. I used rectangles for the sky and ground, circles for the moon and stars, and polygons for the mountains and trees. I also used lines for the tree trunks, an ellipse for the lake, and a polyline for the reflection in the water. The dark colors make the scene feel like nighttime, while the bright moon and stars add contrast.

Reflection: Creating my Midnight Mountain Scene helped me understand how SVG shapes work together to make a picture. The hardest shape to position was the polygon because I had to figure out the correct points to make the mountains look realistic. I learned that changing the numbers can move the shapes or change their sizes. For my first color combination, I tried a lighter blue sky with bright green mountains, but the colors did not match the nighttime theme I wanted. I decided to use darker blues and grays to make the scene look better. My favorite part was adding the moon and stars because they made the picture feel more complete. Outside of class, I have seen similar mountain designs used in website logos, outdoor clothing brands, and travel websites. This assignment helped me understand how SVG can create designs without needing regular image files.
